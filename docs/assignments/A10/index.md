# A10 – Lead Screw Translating System

2157 Section - FEA Analysis and Drawing -- Kelsey Skipper

## Objective

The objective of my portion of this group assignment was to perform Finite Element Analysis (FEA) on the tensile tester crosshead using SolidWorks Simulation. I evaluated the crosshead under a 2,000 N axial load to determine its stress, displacement, and factor of safety. The design requirements included a minimum factor of safety of 4 and a maximum vertical deflection of 0.1 mm.

I was also responsible for creating a detailed engineering drawing of the crosshead with appropriate dimensions, tolerances, and a title block.

## Analyze

I began by importing the team's tensile tester assembly into SolidWorks and creating a static simulation study. I applied a 2,000 N downward force to the crosshead and selected the two lead screw/nut mounting regions as fixed supports.
